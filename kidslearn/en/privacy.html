Gugu Academy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-29　　Version: 1.0

Gugu Academy (咕咕学堂, the "App") is an English-learning app for children aged 5–7. We know privacy is what parents care about most, so the App is designed to collect no personal information at all. This policy also serves as the App's children's privacy notice.

In one sentence

The App needs no account, makes no network connections, shows no ads, contains no third-party analytics or other third-party code, and does not collect, upload or share any personal information about children or parents. Learning records stay on your device.

1. Who we are

The App is developed and provided by the Gugu Academy team ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, please e-mail r23299116@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect, ask for or read any of the following:

  • Name, date of birth, age, gender, photos, voice, school, address, phone number, e-mail or other personal information (during setup the child only picks an animal avatar; no name or birthday is entered);
  • Location, contacts, photo library, camera or microphone (the App requests no system permissions);
  • Device identifiers, the advertising identifier (IDFA), or anything that could identify or track you or your device;
  • Usage data: we do no analytics, no personalized recommendations and no profiling.

The App has no accounts, sign-in, chat, sharing or leaderboards, and no advertising.

3. Information stored only on your device

So that your child can pick up where they left off, the App stores the following locally on your device:

  • Settings: daily time limit, quiet hours, eye-rest breaks, amount of Chinese guidance, volume;
  • The number of the avatar your child picked;
  • Learning progress and records: completed lessons, stars, stickers and where they were placed, each word's review schedule and answer counts;
  • Daily usage time for the last 30 days (for time limits and the parent report).

This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see or access it. You can delete it at any time with "Reset progress" in the parent area, or by deleting the App.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, the system may include this local information in your backup according to your settings. That is handled by Apple and your settings; we cannot see it either.

4. Network

Version 1.0 of the App makes no network requests. Everything works offline.

The "Feedback e-mail" and web links in the parent area open only after the parental gate. "Feedback e-mail" opens the Mail app on your device, and you decide whether and what to send; web links open our web pages in the system browser.

5. Age range (a system feature provided by Apple)

On iOS / iPadOS 26 or later, during first-time setup the App may use Apple's Declared Age Range feature, which asks you whether you want to share an age range with the App. We do this only to comply with legal requirements in some countries and regions (for example, some US states):

  • The result is used only temporarily while the App is running; it is not stored, uploaded or shared;
  • Whatever the result, your child sees exactly the same content;
  • You may choose not to share, and the App works as usual.

6. Crash reports

If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics "with App Developers", Apple may provide us with crash reports for the App. These reports are processed by Apple and do not identify you; we use them only to fix problems.

7. Third parties

The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics, sign-in or other third-party code (SDKs), and provides no information to any third party. Version 1.0 of the App has no paid features.

8. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR) and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code:

  • We collect no personal information from children, so we do not need, and will never ask for, parents' personal information to "verify consent";
  • High privacy by default: no accounts, no network, no ads or tracking, no profiling;
  • No nudging design: no push notifications, no leaderboards, no random rewards, no time-limited events;
  • Settings and external links are only available after the parental gate;
  • Parents' rights: because we keep no information about any child, there is no data to review, correct or delete; learning records on the device are fully under the parent's control (reset them in the parent area, or delete the App).

Please do not let children e-mail us. If we learn that we have received a child's personal information, we will delete it immediately.

9. E-mails you send us

If you contact us by e-mail, we receive your e-mail address and message. We use them only to reply and to help with your question, for no other purpose, and we never give them to any third party. Once your question is resolved, you may ask us to delete them.

10. Changes to this policy

If a change in the App's features affects this policy (for example, adding paid features in the future), we will update this policy and its effective date before the new version is released, and let you know in the App's parent area. If a change affects how children's information is handled, we will obtain parental consent in advance where the law requires it.

11. Contact us

E-mail: r23299116@gmail.com We usually reply within 7 business days.
