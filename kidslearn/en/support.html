Frequently asked questions

1. There's no sound. What should I do? - Press the volume-up button on the side of the device. Gugu Academy plays sound even when the iPhone's silent switch is on, so no sound usually means the volume is too low. - Open the parent area → Settings (设置) and check that the voice, sound-effect and music volumes aren't turned all the way down. - Check whether the device is connected to Bluetooth headphones or a speaker. - After a phone call or switching back from another app, the current prompt is not replayed automatically: tap the speaker button (replay) at the top right, or tap the picture, to hear it again. - Still no sound? Fully close Gugu Academy and open it again; if that doesn't help, restart the device.

2. Where is progress saved? Will it be lost if I switch devices or delete the app? - Progress is saved automatically on this device after each part of a lesson. If your child leaves in the middle or the app is closed, the next session continues from the unfinished part. - Gugu Academy has no account and makes no network connections, so progress does not sync to other devices. Deleting the app also deletes the progress; on a new device your child starts again. - If you use iCloud Backup or back up the whole device to a computer, progress may be restored along with a device backup (this is handled by Apple's backup feature).

3. Where is the parent area and how do I get in? - Tap the small gray gear at the top-right of the lesson map. A two-digit addition written in Chinese numerals appears (for example "三十七 加 二十五" = 37 + 25); type the answer (62) on the number pad. - Chinese numerals: 一 1, 二 2, 三 3, 四 4, 五 5, 六 6, 七 7, 八 8, 九 9, 十 10. "三十七" means 3 × 10 + 7 = 37. - You won't be asked again within 5 minutes; after 3 wrong answers, wait 30 seconds. - The gear is also on the bedtime and quiet-hours screens.

4. How do I set how long my child can use the app each day? - Parent area → Settings (设置) → Daily time (每日时长): 10, 15, 20, 30 or 40 minutes; the default is 15 minutes. - Gugu gives a reminder 2 minutes before the end. When time is up, Gugu waits until your child finishes the current activity and then goes to sleep; the child area is closed for the rest of the day. - In the parent area you can choose "10 more minutes today" (今天再玩 10 分钟), once per day. - After 20 minutes of continuous use, Gugu leads a 30-second eye-rest break (this can be turned off in Settings).

5. When my child opens the app, Gugu is asleep. - These are quiet hours: 9 pm to 7 am by default (device time). Parents can change them in parent area → Settings, but 10 pm to 6 am is always unavailable for children's rest and cannot be turned off. - Or today's time limit has been used up; see question 4.

6. Later lessons won't open. - Lessons unlock in order: finishing one lesson opens the next, and finished lessons can be replayed any time. - To let your child go to any lesson: parent area → Settings → Unlock all lessons (解锁全部课程).

7. On iPad in portrait, the picture becomes smaller. - Gugu Academy is designed for landscape. When the iPad is held upright, the picture is scaled down and centered and Gugu reminds your child to turn the iPad sideways; turn it and the full screen comes back.

8. On first launch, the system asks whether to share an age range with the app. - This is a system feature from Apple (iOS / iPadOS 26 and later). We use it only to meet legal requirements in some regions; the result is not stored or uploaded and does not change what your child sees. You can choose not to share and the app works normally.

9. Does the app need the internet? Does it collect data? - No internet is needed; everything works offline. - Gugu Academy has no ads and no account, and collects no information about children or parents. See the Privacy Policy.

10. How do I clear my child's learning records? - Parent area → Settings → Reset progress (重置学习进度; you'll be asked to confirm), or delete the app.

11. Does it cost anything? - Everything in the current version is available right away. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.
