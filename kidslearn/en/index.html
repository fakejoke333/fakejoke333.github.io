Gugu Academy

Gugu the little owl guides children through English in Mandarin — listen, speak and practice, even before they can read.

For Mandarin-speaking children aged 5–7 · iPhone and iPad · The app's interface and voice guidance are in Simplified Chinese

Download on the App Store

Why Gugu Academy

  1. Mandarin voice guidance all the way Gugu explains every step in Mandarin and shows what to do, so children who can't read yet can learn on their own.
  2. Listen first, then speak, then practice Each new word comes with a picture and clear American English, heard twice, then children say it too. They practice by listening and picking, popping bubbles, dragging and matching, memory cards and finding things in a picture from a spoken sentence. Learned words come back for review on the following days.
  3. Mistakes are fine No scores, no countdowns, no fail screens. If your child gets it wrong, Gugu repeats and shows the answer.
  4. Parents stay in control Daily time limit, quiet hours and eye-rest breaks are set by parents. The learning report shows which words your child has learned and remembered, with simple activities to do together.
  5. Privacy you can trust No ads, no account, no internet connection, and no information collected about children or parents.

What's inside

  • 4 everyday themes — Hello, Colors, Numbers, Animals — plus 1 review park: 13 lessons of 5–8 minutes
  • About 40 everyday English words, the names and sounds of letters A–H, 4 simple sentence patterns
  • 4 unit chants and the ABC song
  • A sticker page for every finished unit and a word card for every new word

Who it's for

  • Mandarin-speaking children aged 5–7 (kindergarten to first grade)
  • Complete beginners start from lesson one; children who already know some English build on it through review and practice
  • The app's interface and voice guidance are in Simplified Chinese (Mandarin)

Our promise

  • No ads, no third-party analytics, no personalized recommendations
  • No account needed; we collect no information about children or parents
  • Works fully offline; learning records stay on your device
  • Settings and external links are behind a parental gate
